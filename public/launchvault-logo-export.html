LaunchVault — Logo & Social Kit

Click any button to download a ready-to-use PNG. The square ones are sized for Instagram (1080×1080).

How to use:
• Instagram profile pic → download "Profile / App icon (square)". It's full-bleed so the circular crop looks perfect.
• Instagram post / story → "Branded square (post)".
• Website / email header, Linktree → "Full logo (light or dark)".
• The .svg files in this folder are the vector originals — infinitely scalable, hand them to any designer/printer.
